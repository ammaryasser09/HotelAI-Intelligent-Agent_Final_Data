NILE PEARL HOTEL
COMPLAINTS AND RESOLUTION
1. Complaint Filing

How to File a Complaint:
Guests can contact the front desk or customer service to file a complaint.

Immediate Issues:
For immediate issues, contact reception (available 24/7).

Post-Stay Complaints:
Complaints after checkout can be submitted through customer service.

Email Complaints:
Detailed complaints can be sent to the hotel's customer service email.

COMPLAINTS AND RESOLUTION
2. Complaint Categories

Room Quality Issues:
Issues with room condition, cleanliness, or amenities.

Service Issues:
Problems with hotel staff service or responsiveness.

Facility Problems:
Issues with hotel facilities such as Wi-Fi, heating, or plumbing.

Billing Disputes:
Concerns about charges or unexpected fees.

Booking Issues:
Problems related to reservation accuracy or modifications.

COMPLAINTS AND RESOLUTION
3. Resolution Process

Issue Assessment:
The hotel will assess the complaint and determine the appropriate resolution.

Prompt Response:
Guest complaints are prioritized and addressed promptly.

Resolution Options:
Solutions may include room changes, service compensation, or refunds.

Escalation:
Unresolved issues may be escalated to management.

COMPLAINTS AND RESOLUTION
4. Service Recovery

Room Changes:
Guests experiencing room issues may be offered alternative accommodation.

Compensation:
The hotel may offer compensation for service failures.

Follow-up:
The hotel may follow up with guests after complaint resolution.

Future Accommodation:
Significant service failures may result in future stay discounts.

COMPLAINTS AND RESOLUTION
5. Complaint Documentation

Complaint Records:
All complaints are documented for service improvement.

Guest Contact Information:
The hotel maintains contact details for follow-up communication.

Resolution Tracking:
The hotel tracks complaint resolution status.

COMPLAINTS AND RESOLUTION
6. Important Information

The current hotel knowledge base does not specify detailed compensation amounts for various complaint types.

Guests are encouraged to report issues during their stay for prompt resolution.

Contact customer service for information about specific complaints.
