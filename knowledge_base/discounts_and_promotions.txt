NILE PEARL HOTEL
DISCOUNTS AND PROMOTIONS

1. Special Rates

Non-refundable rate: 15% below the Flexible rate
Early booking (30 or more days ahead): 15% off
Extended stay (7 or more nights): 12% off
Last-minute (within 7 days): up to 25% off, when offered
Loyalty member rate: 15% off
Corporate rate: 13% off for companies with an agreement
Group rate (10 or more rooms): 18% off
Low-season promotions: up to 20% off, when offered

2. Promotional Codes

Codes are entered when booking. Each code has validity dates and a usage limit; an expired, not-yet-valid or fully used code cannot be applied. Customer service can check whether a code is valid.

3. Terms

Only one discount, code or special rate applies to a booking. Offers are subject to availability and may have blackout dates.
