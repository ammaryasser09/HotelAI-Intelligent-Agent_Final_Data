NILE PEARL HOTEL
BOOKING MODIFICATION POLICY

1. Reservation Changes

A Flexible reservation can be changed free of charge until 48 hours before arrival, as long as a suitable room is free. Non-refundable reservations cannot be changed. If no suitable room is free, the reservation stays exactly as it is.

2. Changes to Check-in or Check-out Dates

New dates need a free room of the same type for every night. The agreed nightly rate is kept. A date change made less than 48 hours before arrival is treated as a late cancellation of the original dates, and the first night is charged.

3. Changes to Room Type

A room type change needs a free room of the new type for the whole stay. The new room type's standard rate applies to the whole stay.

4. Changes to Number of Guests

The room must hold the new number of guests; children count toward capacity. If it does not, a larger room type must be booked.

5. Extending or Shortening a Stay

Extra nights need the same room to be free, so the guest does not have to change rooms; they are charged at the agreed nightly rate. A stay can be shortened free of charge until 48 hours before arrival. A stay shortened after check-in without notice at check-in is charged one extra night as an early departure fee.

6. Payment After a Change

The first-night deposit already paid stays as it is. Any difference in the total is added to, or taken from, the balance paid at the hotel.

7. Guest Name Changes

The guest name can be changed free of charge until 48 hours before arrival. The new guest must present valid identification at check-in.

8. Bookings Made Through Booking Platforms

Reservations made through a booking platform must be changed or cancelled through that platform, under its terms.

9. How to Make a Change

Log in on the hotel website and ask the voice agent: it checks availability, tells you the new total, and makes the change as soon as you agree. Customer service can also make changes by phone on +20 2 2794 5501 or by email to reservations@nilepearlhotel.example, after the identity check in Reservation Security. Every change is confirmed by email.
