NILE PEARL HOTEL
BOOKING MODIFICATION POLICY

1. Reservation Changes

Guests can change a Flexible reservation free of charge until 48 hours before arrival, subject to availability and to any difference in rate. Non-refundable reservations cannot be changed.

2. Changes to Check-in or Check-out Dates

New dates are subject to availability and are charged at the rate for those dates. A date change made less than 48 hours before arrival is treated as a late cancellation of the original dates, and the first night is charged.

3. Changes to Room Type

A room type change is subject to availability; the new room type's rate applies from the date of the change.

4. Changes to Number of Guests

The room must hold the new number of guests; children count toward capacity. If it does not, a larger room type must be booked.

5. Extending or Shortening a Stay

Extra nights are subject to availability. A stay shortened after check-in without notice at check-in is charged one extra night as an early departure fee.

6. Guest Name Changes

The guest name can be changed free of charge until 48 hours before arrival. The new guest must present valid identification at check-in.

7. Bookings Made Through Booking Platforms

Reservations made through a booking platform must be changed or cancelled through that platform, under its terms.

8. How to Request a Change

Contact customer service by phone on +20 2 2794 5501, by email to reservations@nilepearlhotel.example, or through the hotel website. Have the booking reference ready; the identity check in Reservation Security applies.
