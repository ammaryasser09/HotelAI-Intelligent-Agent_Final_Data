NILE PEARL HOTEL
IDENTIFICATION POLICY

1. Overview

All adult guests must present valid identification at check-in.

2. Accepted Documents

Egyptian nationals: national ID card or passport. Foreign nationals: passport. The hotel registers guests as required by Egyptian regulations.

3. Minimum Age

The guest checking in must be at least 18 years old. Guests under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.

4. Questions

Guests with questions about identification can contact reception.
