NILE PEARL HOTEL
HOTEL INFORMATION

1. Overview

Hotel Name:
Nile Pearl Hotel

Location:
14 Corniche El Nil Street, Garden City, Cairo, Egypt

Hotel Category:
5-Star Hotel

Rooms:
150 rooms and suites on 15 guest floors (floors 2 to 16). Many rooms face the Nile; all Suites have a Nile view.

Check-in:
3:00 PM

Check-out:
12:00 PM

Reception:
24/7

Breakfast:
7:00 AM - 10:30 AM, 1,200 EGP per adult

Free Wi-Fi:
Available in all rooms and public areas.

Parking:
Free for hotel guests, with valet service.

Swimming Pool:
Available from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM.

Gym:
Open 24/7, free for hotel guests.

Airport Transfer:
Available on request from 2,500 EGP per trip.

Room Service:
Available 24/7.

Pets:
Pets are not allowed. Service animals are welcome.

Languages Supported:
Arabic, English, French, Russian.

2. Prices

All prices are in Egyptian Pounds (EGP) and include the 12% service charge, 14% VAT and 1% city tax.
