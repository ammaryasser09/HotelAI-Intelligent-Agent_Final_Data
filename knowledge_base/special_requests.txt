NILE PEARL HOTEL
SPECIAL REQUESTS

1. Room Preferences

Guests can request a high floor, a quiet room away from the elevators or a Nile view. Preferences are free and subject to availability at check-in; a guaranteed Nile view costs 2,000 EGP per night.

2. Accessible Rooms

Rooms adapted for guests with mobility needs are available and should be requested when booking.

3. Dietary Requirements

The kitchen prepares vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free meals for breakfast, the restaurant and room service. Guests should report food allergies when booking or at check-in.

4. Celebrations

Room decoration for a birthday, anniversary or honeymoon costs 3,000 EGP. A celebration cake costs from 1,500 EGP and must be ordered 24 hours ahead.

5. Late Arrival

Guests arriving after midnight should tell the hotel in advance so that the booking is not recorded as a no-show.

6. How Requests Are Handled

Requests can be made when booking or later through customer service. The hotel confirms each request; requests are subject to availability.
