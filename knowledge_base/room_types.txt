7. Views

Rooms face either the Nile or the city. A Nile view can be requested free of charge, subject to availability at check-in. A guaranteed Nile view costs 2,000 EGP per night. All Suites face the Nile.

8. Rates

Rates are per room per night, room only (breakfast not included), and follow the Flexible rate. All prices are in Egyptian Pounds (EGP) and include the 12% service charge, 14% VAT and 1% city tax.
