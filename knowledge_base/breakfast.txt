NILE PEARL HOTEL
BREAKFAST

1. Overview

Daily breakfast buffet in the Main Restaurant from 7:00 AM to 10:30 AM.

2. Price

1,200 EGP per adult per day; 600 EGP per child aged 6 to 11; free for children under 6. Room rates do not include breakfast unless it was added to the booking.

3. What Is Served

Hot and cold dishes, Egyptian specialities such as foul and taameya, eggs cooked to order, pastries, fresh fruit, juices, tea and coffee. Vegetarian options are always available; vegan and gluten-free options are available on request.

4. Breakfast in the Room

Breakfast can be served in the room through room service at the same price plus the 300 EGP delivery charge.
