NILE PEARL HOTEL
EMERGENCY PROCEDURES
1. Emergency Contact

Reception:
Contact hotel reception immediately (available 24/7).

Emergency Telephone:
Use the telephone in your room to contact reception.

Front Desk:
The front desk can provide immediate assistance.

Staff Assistance:
Notify any hotel staff member of emergency situations.

EMERGENCY PROCEDURES
2. Medical Emergencies

Serious Illness:
Contact reception immediately for medical assistance.

Injury:
Contact reception for immediate first aid and medical services.

Allergic Reaction:
Inform staff immediately of allergic reactions.

Medical Services Arrangement:
The hotel can arrange emergency medical services.

Hospital Contact:
The hotel can contact hospitals and medical facilities.

Doctor Availability:
The hotel can arrange doctor consultation if needed.

EMERGENCY PROCEDURES
3. Fire Emergency

Alarm System:
Fire alarm systems are monitored 24/7.

Emergency Exits:
Emergency exit routes are clearly marked.

Evacuation Procedures:
Follow staff instructions for evacuation.

Assembly Point:
Guests will be directed to assembly points away from the building.

Stairwell Use:
Use stairs for evacuation (do not use elevators).

Reentry Procedures:
Wait for staff instructions before reentering the building.

EMERGENCY PROCEDURES
4. Security Issues

Theft or Loss:
Report to reception or front desk immediately.

Lost Items:
Contact the front desk for lost and found services.

Suspicious Activity:
Report suspicious behavior to hotel staff.

Room Security:
Lock doors and use room safes for valuables.

EMERGENCY PROCEDURES
5. Environmental Issues

Power Outage:
Contact reception for assistance during power failures.

Water Problems:
Report water issues to maintenance immediately.

HVAC Issues:
Report heating or cooling problems immediately.

Damage to Room:
Report any room damage to reception.

EMERGENCY PROCEDURES
6. Guest-Related Issues

Noise Disturbance:
Contact reception to report noise disturbances.

Unsafe Behavior:
Report unsafe guest behavior to staff.

Conflict Resolution:
Staff can assist with guest conflicts.

EMERGENCY PROCEDURES
7. Emergency Evacuation

When to Evacuate:
Follow staff instructions for evacuation.

Do Not Use Elevators:
Always use stairs during evacuation.

Assist Others:
Help other guests if safely possible.

Report Location:
Report your location to staff at assembly points.

Wait for Instructions:
Wait for staff instructions before returning to rooms.

EMERGENCY PROCEDURES
8. Emergency Contact Information

Reception:
Available 24/7 for all emergencies.

Security:
Security personnel are on-site at all times.

Management:
Hotel management can be contacted for serious issues.

External Services:
The hotel can contact police, fire, or ambulance services.

EMERGENCY PROCEDURES
9. Important Information

In life-threatening emergencies, contact emergency services directly.

Guest safety is the hotel's highest priority.

All staff members are trained in emergency procedures.

Emergency drills are conducted regularly.

Follow all instructions from hotel staff during emergencies.
