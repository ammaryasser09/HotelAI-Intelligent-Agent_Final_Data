NILE PEARL HOTEL
LAUNDRY AND DRY CLEANING

1. Price

Laundry and dry cleaning cost 500 EGP per kilogram.

2. Turnaround

Items handed in before 10:00 AM are returned the same day by 7:00 PM; later items are returned the next day. Express service within 4 hours costs 50% extra.

3. How to Request

Use the laundry bag in the room and call housekeeping, reception or customer service for collection.
