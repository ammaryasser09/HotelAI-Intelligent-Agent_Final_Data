5. Contact Channels

Reception:
Available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Handles emergencies, immediate assistance,
check-in, check-out, lost and found, and general requests.

Customer Service:
Handles reservations, booking questions, reservation changes, cancellations and
payment enquiries.

Hotel Management:
Handles escalated complaints and matters requiring a management decision.


6. Talking to a Member of Staff

Guests who have a problem with their booking or stay can ask the hotel's voice agent to talk to a person at any time. The conversation then continues as a live chat with a member of the hotel staff. If no one is free at that moment, the agent opens an urgent ticket and a member of staff contacts the guest as soon as possible, in the chat or by email.

7. Important Information

For life-threatening emergencies, contact the Egyptian emergency services directly
rather than the hotel: ambulance 123, police 122, fire 180.
