NILE PEARL HOTEL
NO-SHOW POLICY

1. Definition

A reservation is recorded as a no-show when the guest does not arrive by the end of the check-in grace period.

2. Grace Period

A confirmed reservation is held until 23:59 on the scheduled check-in date. After that time, if the guest has not arrived and has not contacted the hotel, the reservation status changes from Confirmed to No-show.

3. Effect on the Room

Once a reservation becomes a no-show, the room is released back into availability for the remaining nights. The reservation itself is retained in the booking history and is not deleted.

4. Difference from Cancellation

A cancellation is a reservation the guest withdrew before arrival. A no-show is a reservation the guest neither used nor withdrew. The two are recorded as separate statuses.

5. Avoiding a No-show

Guests who expect to arrive after 23:59 on the check-in date should inform the hotel in advance; the booking is then held. Reception operates 24 hours a day. Guests who no longer need the reservation should cancel it, because a Flexible booking cancelled at least 48 hours ahead is free.

6. Charges

Flexible rate: a no-show is charged the first night; the remaining nights are released and not charged. Non-refundable rate: the full amount is kept.
