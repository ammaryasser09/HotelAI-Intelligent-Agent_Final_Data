NILE PEARL HOTEL
BOOKING CONFIRMATION

1. Confirmation

A reservation is confirmed as soon as its payment link is paid: the first-night deposit on the Flexible rate, or the full amount on the Non-refundable rate. Until then the room is held for 24 hours, or for 2 hours if arrival is less than 24 hours away. If the link is not paid in time, the reservation is cancelled automatically and the room is released. The confirmation is sent by email as soon as the payment is received.

2. Booking Reference

Every reservation has a booking reference made of the letter B followed by six digits, for example B013193. Guests should keep it; customer service asks for it when a booking is changed, cancelled or checked.

3. Confirmation Details

The confirmation shows the guest name, number of guests, check-in and check-out dates, room type, rate plan, total price and the cancellation terms. For the Flexible rate it also shows the first-night deposit and the payment link.

4. Missing Confirmation

If the confirmation does not arrive within one hour, check the spam folder, then contact customer service with the guest name and dates so the booking can be found and the confirmation resent.
