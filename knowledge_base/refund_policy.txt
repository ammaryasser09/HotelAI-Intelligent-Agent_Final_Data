NILE PEARL HOTEL
REFUND POLICY

1. Refund Eligibility

Flexible bookings cancelled at least 48 hours before arrival are refunded in full. Flexible bookings cancelled later, or not used, are refunded except for the first night. Non-refundable bookings are not refunded.

2. Refund Processing Time

The hotel processes approved refunds within 3 business days. A card refund usually appears on the statement 7 to 14 business days later, depending on the bank. Bank transfers, including InstaPay, can take up to 14 business days. Cash payments are refunded in cash at reception or by bank transfer.

3. Refund Method

Refunds go back to the original payment method.

4. Incidentals Deposit

The deposit taken at check-in is released at check-out. Cash is returned at once; a card hold is released by the bank within 7 to 14 business days.

5. Refund Enquiries

Guests can ask customer service for the status of a refund with their booking reference.
