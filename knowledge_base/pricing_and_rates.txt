NILE PEARL HOTEL
PRICING AND RATES

1. Room Rates

Single Room: 6,000 EGP per night
Double Room: 8,000 EGP per night
Twin Room: 8,500 EGP per night
Deluxe Room: 12,000 EGP per night
Family Room: 14,000 EGP per night
Suite: 20,000 EGP per night
Guaranteed Nile view: 2,000 EGP per night

Rates are room only and follow the Flexible rate. Nightly rates vary with season and demand, so the rate for specific dates is confirmed when booking.

2. Rate Plans

Flexible rate: free cancellation until 48 hours before arrival; the first night is paid online as a deposit through a payment link and the rest is paid at the hotel.
Non-refundable rate: 15% below the Flexible rate; paid in full at booking; cannot be cancelled, changed or refunded.
Member, corporate, group and seasonal rates are listed in Discounts and Promotions. Only one discount or special rate applies to a booking.

3. Service Pricing

Breakfast buffet: 1,200 EGP per adult per day; 600 EGP per child aged 6 to 11; free for children under 6
Airport transfer: 2,500 EGP per trip by sedan (up to 3 guests); 4,000 EGP by minivan (up to 6 guests)
Laundry and dry cleaning: 500 EGP per kilogram
Spa: 3,500 EGP for a 60-minute treatment; 4,800 EGP for 90 minutes
Gym: free for hotel guests
Room service: 300 EGP delivery charge per order, plus the menu price of each item
Extra bed: 2,500 EGP per night
Late check-out until 4:00 PM: 1,500 EGP

4. Taxes and Currency

All prices are in Egyptian Pounds (EGP) and include the 12% service charge, 14% VAT and 1% city tax. Payments are made in Egyptian Pounds; cards issued abroad are charged in EGP and converted by the card issuer.

5. Calculating a Stay

The room total is the nightly rate multiplied by the number of nights. Services are added at the prices above. Example: a Twin Room for three nights at the standard rate is 3 x 8,500 = 25,500 EGP.
