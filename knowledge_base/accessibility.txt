NILE PEARL HOTEL
ACCESSIBILITY
1. Accessible Facilities

Accessible Rooms:
Rooms suitable for guests with mobility requirements may be available.

Wheelchair Access:
The hotel provides wheelchair-accessible rooms and areas.

Elevator Access:
Elevators are available for guest access to all floors.

Accessible Bathrooms:
Accessible bathrooms with grab bars and appropriate fixtures are available.

Wide Doorways:
Accessible rooms feature appropriately wide doorways.

ACCESSIBILITY
2. Accessibility Services

Assistance Services:
Staff can provide assistance to guests with mobility challenges.

Room Features:
Accessible rooms include appropriate furniture and equipment placement.

Bathroom Equipment:
Grab bars, handheld showers, and accessible toilets are available.

Communication Assistance:
The hotel can arrange communication assistance if needed.

ACCESSIBILITY
3. Visual Accessibility

Accessible Signage:
Guest navigation signage is clearly marked.

Lighting:
Appropriate lighting is provided throughout the hotel.

Audio Assistance:
Audio descriptions may be available for select amenities.

ACCESSIBILITY
4. Hearing Accessibility

Visual Alerts:
Visual alert systems are available in accessible rooms.

Communication Methods:
Multiple communication methods are available for deaf and hard of hearing guests.

Interpretation Services:
Interpretation services may be available upon request.

ACCESSIBILITY
5. Other Accessibility Features

Parking:
Accessible parking spaces are available for guests.

Restaurant Access:
The restaurant and dining areas are accessible to guests with mobility requirements.

Public Areas:
Common areas are accessible to all guests.

Elevator Availability:
Elevators provide access to all guest floors.

ACCESSIBILITY
6. Service Animals

Service Animals:
Service animals assisting guests may be accommodated.

Documentation:
Service animal documentation may be required.

Room Assignment:
Rooms accommodating service animals can be arranged.

ACCESSIBILITY
7. Booking and Assistance

Accessibility Requests:
Guests can request accessible accommodations during booking.

Special Requirements:
Guests can specify accessibility requirements during reservation.

Staff Support:
Staff are trained to assist guests with accessibility needs.

Contact:
Guests should contact customer service with specific accessibility questions.

ACCESSIBILITY
8. Important Information

The current hotel knowledge base does not specify all specific accessibility features in detail.

For complete information about accessibility features, contact customer service during booking.

The hotel is committed to accommodating guests with accessibility requirements.
