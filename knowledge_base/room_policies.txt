NILE PEARL HOTEL
ROOM POLICIES

1. Room Capacity

Single Room:
Maximum capacity: 1 guest.

Double Room:
Maximum capacity: 2 guests.

Twin Room:
Maximum capacity: 2 guests.

Deluxe Room:
Maximum capacity: 3 guests.

Family Room:
Maximum capacity: 4 guests.

Suite:
Maximum capacity: 4 guests.


2. Extra Accommodation

Extra beds or additional accommodation for children are subject to room capacity and availability.

An extra bed can be requested for 2,500 EGP per night. It does not raise the room capacity. A baby cot is free on request.


3. Children

Children are welcome at the hotel.

Additional accommodation for children is subject to room capacity and availability.


4. Pets

Pets are not allowed inside the hotel.


5. Smoking

Smoking is not permitted inside guest rooms.

Designated outdoor smoking areas are available. Smoking in a room incurs a cleaning fee of 7,500 EGP.


6. Room Assignment

Rooms are assigned at check-in. Rooms are on floors 2 to 16 and face the Nile or the city. A high floor, a quiet room or a Nile view can be requested free of charge, subject to availability; a guaranteed Nile view costs 2,000 EGP per night.


7. Room Amenities

Room amenities may vary depending on the selected room type.


8. Guest Responsibility

Guests are expected to respect hotel property and room facilities.

Damage to hotel property is charged at repair or replacement cost.


Guest Capacity Rule

Children count toward room capacity. The total number of guests, adults and children
together, must not exceed the capacity of the selected room type.

A party larger than four guests requires more than one room, because no room type at
Nile Pearl Hotel holds more than four guests.
