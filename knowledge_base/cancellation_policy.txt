NILE PEARL HOTEL
CANCELLATION POLICY

1. Flexible Rate

Cancellation is free until 48 hours before 3:00 PM on the arrival date, and the first-night deposit is refunded in full. A later cancellation is charged the first night: the deposit is kept and the remaining nights are not charged.

2. Non-refundable Rate

Non-refundable reservations are paid in full at booking and are not refunded if cancelled, changed or not used.

3. Group Bookings

Groups of 10 or more rooms can release rooms free of charge until 30 days before arrival. After that the 30% deposit is non-refundable.

4. Already Cancelled or Completed Reservations

A cancelled reservation cannot be cancelled again. A completed stay cannot be cancelled; concerns about a completed stay are handled as complaints.

5. Early Departure

Leaving earlier than booked without telling reception at check-in is charged one extra night.

6. How to Cancel

Contact customer service with the booking reference. Bookings made through a booking platform are cancelled through that platform. A cancellation confirmation is sent by email.
