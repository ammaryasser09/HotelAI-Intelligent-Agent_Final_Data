NILE PEARL HOTEL
LOCATION AND SURROUNDINGS

1. Address

Nile Pearl Hotel, 14 Corniche El Nil Street, Garden City, Cairo, Egypt. The hotel stands on the east bank of the Nile in Garden City, a quiet embassy district in central Cairo.

2. Cairo International Airport

The airport is about 25 km from the hotel. The drive usually takes 35 to 60 minutes depending on traffic. The hotel airport transfer costs 2,500 EGP per trip by sedan (up to 3 guests) or 4,000 EGP by minivan (up to 6 guests) and should be booked at least 24 hours in advance.

3. Nearby Landmarks

- Tahrir Square and the Egyptian Museum: about 2 km, 10 minutes by car
- Cairo Tower and Zamalek: about 3 km, 10 to 15 minutes by car
- Khan el-Khalili bazaar and Al-Azhar: about 5 km, 20 minutes by car
- Giza Pyramids: about 17 km, 40 to 60 minutes by car
- Grand Egyptian Museum: about 18 km, 40 to 60 minutes by car

4. Getting Around

The nearest metro stations are Saad Zaghloul and Sadat (Tahrir), both within about 1 km. Reception can call a taxi or a ride-hailing car at any hour. The concierge can recommend tours and restaurants; tour bookings are made with outside operators.

5. Everyday Needs

Pharmacies, ATMs, a supermarket and currency exchange offices are within a 5 to 10 minute walk in Garden City. Reception can give directions at any hour, call a taxi, or arrange pharmacy delivery to the room. For a medical emergency, call reception immediately.
