NILE PEARL HOTEL
AIRPORT TRANSFER

1. Service

Private pickup and drop-off between Cairo International Airport and the hotel.

2. Prices

Sedan, up to 3 guests with luggage: 2,500 EGP per trip. Minivan, up to 6 guests: 4,000 EGP per trip.

3. Booking

Book at least 24 hours in advance with the flight number and arrival time. The driver meets arriving guests in the arrivals hall with a name sign and waits up to 60 minutes after landing at no extra charge.

4. Journey Time

The airport is about 25 km away; the journey takes 35 to 60 minutes depending on traffic.
