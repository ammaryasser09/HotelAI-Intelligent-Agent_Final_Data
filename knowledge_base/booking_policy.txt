NILE PEARL HOTEL
BOOKING POLICY

1. Room Reservations

Guests can reserve any available room. Availability depends on the selected dates and room type. Guests should give accurate booking information.

2. Booking Channels

Reservations can be made on the hotel website, by phone on +20 2 2794 5501, by email to reservations@nilepearlhotel.example, through booking platforms, or at reception.

3. Information Required

A reservation requires the guest's full name, number of adults and children, check-in and check-out dates, room type, phone number and email address. On the Flexible rate the first night is paid online as a deposit through a secure payment link sent after booking, and the rest is paid at the hotel; the Non-refundable rate is paid in full at booking.

4. Room Types and Capacity

Single Room: 1 guest
Double Room: 2 guests
Twin Room: 2 guests
Deluxe Room: 3 guests
Family Room: 4 guests
Suite: 4 guests

Children count toward room capacity. An extra bed costs 2,500 EGP per night and does not raise the room capacity.

5. Group Bookings

Bookings of 10 or more rooms receive the group rate, 18% below the Flexible rate. A 30% deposit is due at confirmation and the balance 7 days before arrival. Rooms can be released free of charge until 30 days before arrival; after that the deposit is non-refundable.

6. Minimum Age

The guest checking in must be at least 18 years old. Guests under 18 must stay with a parent or guardian.
