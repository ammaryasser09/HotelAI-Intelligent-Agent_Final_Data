NILE PEARL HOTEL
LOYALTY PROGRAMS

1. Nile Pearl Rewards

The hotel loyalty programme is free to join during booking, at check-in or on the hotel website.

2. Membership Tiers

Standard: on joining
Silver: 10 nights in a calendar year
Gold: 25 nights in a calendar year
Platinum: 50 nights in a calendar year
A tier is kept for the following calendar year.

3. Earning Points

Members earn 1 point for every 10 EGP spent on rooms and hotel services. Tier bonus: Silver +20%, Gold +50%, Platinum +100%.

4. Using Points

1,000 points give a 500 EGP discount on rooms or hotel services. Points expire after 24 months without earning or using points. Points cannot be transferred to another person.

5. Tier Benefits

Silver: late check-out until 1:00 PM
Gold: late check-out until 2:00 PM, room upgrade on arrival and 10% off spa treatments
Platinum: late check-out until 4:00 PM, free breakfast for two, priority upgrades including Suites, and 15% off spa treatments
Late check-out and upgrades are subject to availability.

6. Member Rate and Combining Offers

Members can book the Loyalty Member rate, 15% below the Flexible rate. Only one discount applies to a booking, so the member rate cannot be combined with corporate, group or promotional rates; the best applicable rate is used.

7. Referrals

A member earns 1,000 bonus points when a new guest they refer completes a first stay.
