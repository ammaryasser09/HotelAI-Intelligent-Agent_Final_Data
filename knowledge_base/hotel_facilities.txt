NILE PEARL HOTEL
HOTEL FACILITIES

1. Swimming Pool

Open 7:00 AM - 9:00 PM for hotel guests. Towels are provided at the pool. Children must be supervised.

2. Gym

Open 24/7 and free for hotel guests: cardio machines and free weights. Guests under 16 must be accompanied by an adult.

3. Wi-Fi

Free in all rooms and public areas, 24/7. The network name and password are printed on the key-card holder given at check-in.

4. Parking

Free for hotel guests, with valet service at the main entrance 24/7.

5. Reception

Open 24/7 for check-in, check-out, general requests, hotel services and emergencies.

6. Room Service

24/7. A 300 EGP delivery charge applies per order, plus menu prices.

7. Restaurant and Breakfast

Main Restaurant: breakfast 7:00 AM - 10:30 AM (1,200 EGP per adult), lunch 12:30 PM - 4:00 PM, dinner 7:00 PM - 11:00 PM.

8. Spa

Open 9:00 AM - 10:00 PM. A 60-minute treatment costs 3,500 EGP.

9. Airport Transfer

Private transfer on request: 2,500 EGP per trip by sedan (up to 3 guests), 4,000 EGP by minivan (up to 6 guests). Book at least 24 hours in advance.
