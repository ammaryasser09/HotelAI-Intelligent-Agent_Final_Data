NILE PEARL HOTEL
CANCELLATION PROCESS

1. Provide Reservation Details

The guest gives the booking reference and the last name on the booking.

2. Check the Rate Plan

Customer service checks whether the booking is Flexible or Non-refundable and whether the 48-hour deadline has passed.

3. Confirm the Charges

The guest is told before cancelling whether the cancellation is free, costs the first night, or is non-refundable.

4. Process the Cancellation

The booking is cancelled and the room released.

5. Process Any Refund

Refunds are processed within 3 business days as described in the Refund Policy.

6. Keep the Confirmation

A cancellation confirmation is sent by email and should be kept for reference.
