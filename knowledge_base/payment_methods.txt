NILE PEARL HOTEL
PAYMENT METHODS

1. Accepted Payment Methods

- Credit and debit cards: Visa, Mastercard and American Express
- Cash in Egyptian Pounds
- Digital wallets: Apple Pay and Vodafone Cash
- Bank transfer, including InstaPay

2. Payment Security

The hotel never asks for full card details by phone, chat, WhatsApp or email. Card details are entered only on the hotel's secure payment page or given in person at reception.

3. Currency

Payments are made in Egyptian Pounds. Cards issued abroad are charged in EGP and converted by the card issuer.

4. Payment Assistance

Guests can contact customer service about payment questions or problems.
