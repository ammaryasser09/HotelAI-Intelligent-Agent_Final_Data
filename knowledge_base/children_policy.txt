NILE PEARL HOTEL
CHILDREN POLICY

1. Overview

Children are welcome at the hotel.

2. Guest Capacity Rule

Children count toward room capacity. The total number of guests, adults and children together, must not exceed the capacity of the selected room type. A party larger than four guests requires more than one room, because no room type at Nile Pearl Hotel holds more than four guests.

3. Children's Rates

Children under 6 stay free in the existing beds and have breakfast free. Children aged 6 to 11 pay 600 EGP for breakfast. Guests aged 12 and over pay adult prices.

4. Beds for Children

A baby cot is free on request. An extra bed costs 2,500 EGP per night and does not raise the room capacity.

5. Facilities

Children must be supervised at the pool. Guests under 16 are not admitted to the spa and must be accompanied by an adult in the gym.
