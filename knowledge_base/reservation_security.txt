NILE PEARL HOTEL
RESERVATION SECURITY

1. Identity Check

Before discussing, changing or cancelling an existing reservation, customer service asks for the booking reference and one of: the last name on the booking, or the last four digits of the phone number on the booking. Details are shared only when both match.

2. What the Hotel Never Asks For

The hotel never asks for full card numbers, card security codes or passwords by phone, chat, WhatsApp or email.

3. Confirmation of Changes

Every change or cancellation is confirmed by email to the address on the booking.

4. Suspicious Contact

Guests who receive a suspicious message claiming to be from the hotel should contact reception on +20 2 2794 5500.
