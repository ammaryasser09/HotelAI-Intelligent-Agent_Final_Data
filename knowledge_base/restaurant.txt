NILE PEARL HOTEL
RESTAURANT

1. Main Restaurant Hours

Breakfast buffet: 7:00 AM - 10:30 AM
Lunch: 12:30 PM - 4:00 PM
Dinner: 7:00 PM - 11:00 PM

2. Menu

Lunch and dinner are served à la carte from the hotel menu: Egyptian and Middle Eastern dishes, grills, pasta, sandwiches and desserts. The full menu with prices is in the In-Room Dining Menu document; prices are the same in the restaurant, without the delivery charge.

3. Dietary Information

All meat served is halal. Dishes are marked for vegetarian (V), gluten (G), dairy (D), egg (E), nuts (N) and fish (F). Guests with allergies should tell the staff when ordering; the kitchen handles allergens with care but cannot guarantee a dish is completely free of traces.

4. Beverages

The hotel does not serve alcoholic beverages.

5. Reservations

Hotel guests do not need a reservation. Groups of 8 or more should book a table through reception.
