NILE PEARL HOTEL
ROOM SERVICE

1. Availability

Room service operates 24 hours a day. The full menu is available from 11:00 AM to 11:00 PM; a night menu is available from 11:00 PM to 6:00 AM; breakfast is served from 7:00 AM to 10:30 AM.

2. Price

Each order has a 300 EGP delivery charge, plus the menu price of each item. The menu with prices is in the In-Room Dining Menu document.

3. Ordering

Order from the room phone, through reception or through customer service. Delivery usually takes 30 to 45 minutes.

4. Payment

Room service is charged to the room or paid on delivery.
