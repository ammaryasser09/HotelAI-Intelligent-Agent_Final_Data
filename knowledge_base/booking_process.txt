NILE PEARL HOTEL
BOOKING PROCESS

1. Select Dates

The guest chooses the check-in and check-out dates.

2. Select Room

The guest chooses a room type that fits the number of guests, and a rate plan: Flexible or Non-refundable.

3. Provide Guest Information

The guest gives full name, number of adults and children, phone number and email address.

4. Review Reservation

The guest checks the dates, room type, number of guests, rate plan and total price.

5. Payment

Flexible: the first night is paid online as a deposit through a secure payment link sent by email right after booking; the rest of the stay is paid at the hotel at check-in or check-out. Non-refundable: the full amount is paid through the payment link at booking. The link must be paid within 24 hours, or within 2 hours if arrival is less than 24 hours away; otherwise the booking is cancelled automatically and the room is released.

6. Confirmation

The booking reference is given at once. The booking is confirmed, and the confirmation sent by email, when the payment link is paid.

7. Changes After Booking

Changes and cancellations are handled by customer service according to the Booking Modification and Cancellation policies.
