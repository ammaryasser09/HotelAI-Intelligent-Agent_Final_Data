NILE PEARL HOTEL
LOST AND FOUND

1. Overview

Items found on hotel property are handled by the Lost and Found department.

2. Reporting a Lost Item

Contact reception with a description of the item and where it was left.

3. Storage

Found items are kept for 90 days; valuables are kept in the hotel safe.

4. Returning Items

Items can be collected at reception with identification, or sent by courier at the guest's expense.
