NILE PEARL HOTEL
PAYMENT POLICY

1. When Payment Is Taken

Flexible rate: the first night is paid online as a deposit through a secure payment link sent after booking, and the rest of the stay is paid at the hotel, at check-in or check-out. Non-refundable rate: the full amount is paid at booking.

2. Incidentals Deposit

Separately from the first-night deposit, at check-in the hotel takes a refundable deposit of 3,000 EGP per night, by card pre-authorisation or in cash, to cover room service and other charges. It is released at check-out.

3. Additional Charges

Services are charged at their listed prices, for example breakfast 1,200 EGP per adult, airport transfer 2,500 EGP per trip, laundry 500 EGP per kilogram and a 60-minute spa treatment 3,500 EGP. Damage to hotel property is charged at repair or replacement cost.

4. Taxes

All prices are in Egyptian Pounds (EGP) and include the 12% service charge, 14% VAT and 1% city tax.

5. Currency

All payments are in Egyptian Pounds. Cards issued abroad are charged in EGP and converted by the card issuer.

6. Payment Problems

If a payment is declined, the guest can use another card or another accepted method through the same payment link, or ask customer service to send a new link; the payment deadline does not change. A booking whose link is not paid within 24 hours (2 hours if arrival is less than 24 hours away) is cancelled automatically. Customer service and reception help with payment problems; billing disputes are handled as complaints.
